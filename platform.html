Core operating contract

A Command is more than a task.

It carries the required outcome, accountable owner, governing process, policy checks, AI authority, approval state, evidence, cost, and final result.

O

Outcome

Define what must be true when the work is complete.

A

Authority

Check role, policy, budget, and approval before execution.

E

Evidence

Attach records that prove work and outcome.

L

Learning

Feed results back into metrics, processes, and decisions.

AI runtime boundary

Vercel never assumes localhost is reachable.

In production, a local NerdCommand AI node makes outbound authenticated connections to the OS, claims signed jobs, checks policy locally, calls Ollama or LM Studio, and returns results with usage and evidence.
