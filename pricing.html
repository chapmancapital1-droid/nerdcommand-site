Commercial structure

Five rights must be explicit.

Qualified counsel must review the final customer agreement before paid launch. Software ownership, customer customization, open-source licenses, generated materials, hosting, and updates are different rights.

01

Source snapshot

Defines the customer's rights to the purchased product version and number of deployments.

02

Updates

Defines access to patches, compatible module versions, new workflows, and migration tooling.

03

Cloud operation

Defines hosting, monitoring, backups, AI usage, support, integrations, and service boundaries.

04

Custom extensions

Defines ownership and maintenance for customer-specific code and data migrations.

05

Partner rights

Defines multi-client, resale, white-label, and support responsibilities for approved partners.

!

No income promise

NerdCommand provides operating software and resources, not guaranteed customers, earnings, or legal permission to operate.
